## 🎯 Frontend Interview Questions (Based on Your Work)

### 🔐 **Authentication & Token Handling**

1. **How do you securely store and manage access tokens on the client?**
2. **Why did you choose to store the refresh token in an HTTP-only cookie instead of localStorage?**
3. **Explain how you implemented session auto-refresh in your project.**
4. **How do you differentiate token-based authentication flows between students and experts?**
5. **What are the security implications of storing access tokens in localStorage? How did you mitigate them?**
6. **How would you prevent CSRF and XSS vulnerabilities in your auth implementation?**

---

### ⚛️ **React Context & Hooks**

7. **How does the `AuthProvider` manage and propagate user state throughout your application?**
8. **Explain the role of `useAuthContext` and how you ensured it was only used within the `AuthProvider`.**
9. **What happens if a component calls `useAuthContext` outside of the provider?**
10. **How do you ensure the context updates when a user logs in, logs out, or refreshes a session?**

---

### 🚀 **Next.js App Routing & Layouts**

11. **How did you structure your application to separate student and expert routes using Next.js layouts?**
12. **Why did you place your `AuthProvider` inside a nested layout rather than the root `_app.tsx`?**
13. **How do you protect routes and ensure only authenticated users can access them?**

---

### 🔑 **Google OAuth Integration**

14. **How did you integrate Google OAuth with role-based logic (student vs expert)?**
15. **What libraries did you use for Google login, and how do you handle the ID token on the client and server?**
16. **Why is the GoogleOAuthProvider placed inside the layout?**

### 🧠 **Design & Architecture**

17. **How did you structure your token refresh logic to minimize latency and avoid token expiry?**
18. **Why did you subtract 20 seconds from the token expiry time before refreshing it?**
19. **What’s your approach to error handling in fetch/post hooks when the token is invalid or expired?**
20. **What’s the benefit of a shared `post()` utility versus writing raw `fetch()` calls?**
